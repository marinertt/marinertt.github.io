About SPARKTACUS!

More capability. Less gatekeeping.

Technology should not require a toga, a trust fund, or a six-figure procurement process.

SPARKTACUS helps organizations make practical use of data engineering, cloud optimization, AI/ML, and automation. I look for ways to reduce waste, make useful capabilities more accessible, and leave your team with something it can understand and maintain.

Why the name?

Spartacus was a Thracian gladiator who led an uprising of enslaved people against the Roman Republic from 73 to 71 BCE. The revolt was ultimately crushed, but his name endured as a symbol of resistance to a system that treated human beings as disposable.

SPARKTACUS borrows that spirit for a modern problem: too much useful technology is treated as if it belongs only to large institutions with deep pockets and the right connections. I believe smaller organizations should be able to benefit from advanced tools, too—without having to play corporate politics just to get a fair shot at them.

I know what it feels like when people mistake a different communication style for a lack of ability. I’m more interested in what you can build, improve, and save than how smoothly you navigate office politics. The work should speak for itself.

What working with me looks like

I’ll help identify where automation, data pipelines, storage choices, or AI/ML could make a real difference. Then I’ll build a practical solution, document how it works, and teach your team how to maintain it and find help when needed.

You get the knowledge and the work product. There’s no forced subscription or requirement to hire a permanent team to keep it running. If you want ongoing help, you can bring me back when you need me.

About Todd

I’m a U.S. Army veteran, born and raised in the Mid-Atlantic. Before working in data and technology, I worked as a linguist in Arabic and German and taught English in South Korea. Travel and language have kept me curious about how people solve problems in very different places.

These days, I work across data engineering, cloud platforms, and automation. Outside of work, I play French horn, keep studying languages, and spend time with Sweetpea, my small dog with a large opinion of the world.
