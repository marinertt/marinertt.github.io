Why SPARKTACUS?

SPARKTACUS is about using technology to make life easier—not turning every problem into a subscription and every customer into a captive.

The name behind the name

Spartacus was an enslaved Thracian gladiator who helped lead a major rebellion against the Roman Republic from 73 to 71 BCE. What began as an escape from a gladiatorial school grew into an uprising involving tens of thousands of enslaved people.

The story behind the emblem

Ancient accounts of Spartacus’s revolt describe a striking reversal of Roman power. Florus writes that the rebels presented Spartacus with insignia and symbols of authority taken from defeated Roman commanders, and that he accepted them. Plutarch recounts that the rebels also armed themselves with weapons captured from Roman forces.

Those accounts do not tell us that Spartacus’s followers combined the insignia of places or units they had left. The connection to my emblem is an interpretation: I was inspired by the idea of taking symbols associated with established institutions and giving them a new purpose.

My shield brings together elements of my own history. Its seven-sided form draws from the insignia of the Jersey Blues, a unit I served with in the New Jersey Army National Guard. The blue and buff connect me to New Jersey and George Washington University. The three red stars represent Washington, DC, where I live and where SPARKTACUS began.

The result is a symbol of where I’ve been and what I’m building: an independent company that puts the experience I gained within established organizations to work helping clients reduce waste, lower costs, and reclaim time for what matters.
