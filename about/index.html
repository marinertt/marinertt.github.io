About SPARKTACUS!

More capability. Less gatekeeping.

Technology should not require a toga, a trust fund, or a six-figure procurement process.

SPARKTACUS helps organizations make practical use of data engineering, cloud optimization, AI/ML, and automation. I look for ways to reduce waste, make useful capabilities more accessible, and leave your team with something it can understand and maintain.

Why SPARKTACUS?

One part ancient revolt. One part modern data revolution. Zero patience for gatekeeping.

Spartacus was a Thracian gladiator who led an uprising against the Roman Republic from 73 to 71 BCE. His revolt was defeated, but his name became a lasting symbol of resistance to a system built to keep power in the hands of a few.

Spark is for Apache Spark: the open-source engine that changed how people could work with very large datasets. Spark began as a research project at UC Berkeley, was open-sourced in 2010, and grew into a community-built platform for large-scale data processing and analytics. It showed that powerful data tools didn’t have to stay locked inside a handful of companies and institutions.

SPARKTACUS brings those ideas together. Useful technology—data engineering, automation, AI/ML, and cost-saving tools—should be within reach of organizations that don’t have a giant budget or an inside track. I help make those capabilities practical, teach your team how to use and maintain what we build, and leave you in control.

The old systems said, “People like you don't belong here.” Spartacus and Spark both suggest a better answer: make your own way in.

What working with me looks like

I’ll help identify where automation, data pipelines, storage choices, or AI/ML could make a real difference. Then I’ll build a practical solution, document how it works, and teach your team how to maintain it and find help when needed.

You get the knowledge and the work product. There’s no forced subscription or requirement to hire a permanent team to keep it running. If you want ongoing help, you can bring me back when you need me.
