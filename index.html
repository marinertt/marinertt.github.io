Start with the part that makes the work harder.

Finding the right version of a document. Keeping track of sources. Paying to store copies you no longer need. A useful technical change should address a problem you can point to.

Keeping what matters

Work out what needs to be kept, how readily you need to reach it, and how much it costs to store. Compare ways to preserve the material without paying for unnecessary copies.

Choosing suitable tools

Find out whether your files and spreadsheets need a few changes or a different home. Compare the expense and upkeep of each option against the work it would save.

Making a manageable change

Plan how to move existing material, check that it arrives intact, and keep it usable. Account for the time, cost, and ongoing care the change will require.
